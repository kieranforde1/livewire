How to play

  • 1Drag your finger from a generator to a town of the same colour to lay a cable.
  • 2Towns show white dots when they need demand. A ring means a pulse is on its way.
  • 3If too many dots wait with no pulse coming, or with their pulse overloaded, the town starts to pulse and a timer fills. You have about a minute to fix it. When it's full, the game ends.
  • 4Cable tiles run out. Each week you get more, and you pick one of three upgrades, like transformers, express lines or a excavator. New tools appear in your toolbar, and tapping one tells you how to use it.
  • 5The crossed-out generator at the top counts generators with no cable to their town. They pulse on the map, and so does any town that no matching generator can reach yet.
  • 6Reach a map's MW target to unlock the next, harder map.
  • 7Cables turn red where pulses are overloaded. That's where a transformer or a new route will help most.
  • 8Made a mistake? The undo button, bottom left, takes back your last cable change.
  • 9Tap a town to pause and see which generators and cables can reach it. When you’re zoomed in, arrows at the edge of the screen point to towns that are filling up.
  • 10Pause any time. You can still draw while paused. Pinch to zoom. If colours are hard to tell apart, turn on colour shapes in the menu.
