How to play

  • 1Pick a power station in the toolbar, then tap open land to build it. Coal plants can’t go within 2 tiles of a town.
  • 2Drag cables to join power stations to towns. Power spreads through every cable it can reach, all at once.
  • 3One strand of cable carries 5 MW. Draw along a cable again to add a strand, up to 3. Busy cables waste power as heat, more the fuller and longer they are, and extra strands cut the waste.
  • 4Cables turn amber, then red, when they carry too much. If one stays red, its breaker trips and its power moves onto other cables, which can trip too. The breaker locks for a few seconds, then you can tap the gap to reset it. A cable that trips badly overloaded, or three times in a row, burns out and is gone.
  • 5The gauge at the top shows power reaching towns against what they need. When a grid is short, every town on it dims and a timer starts. If a town goes fully dark, the game ends.
  • 6Coal runs day and night. Solar runs in daylight. Wind comes and goes, and does better next to water. Batteries store spare power and give it back when the grid is short.
  • 7Towns need the least power at night and the most in the evening, just after sunset.
  • 8Each day brings more cable, new power stations and an upgrade. Tap a town, a power station or a cable to see how it’s doing. Undo is at the bottom left. Pinch to zoom.
